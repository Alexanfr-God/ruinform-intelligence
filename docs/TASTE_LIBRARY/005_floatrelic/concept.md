# FLOATRELIC / MAGNETIC BALANCE

**Card ID:** `005_floatrelic`  
**Status:** curated-v0  
**Design operator:** `Expose invisible force` (`expose_invisible_force`)  
**Difficulty:** EASY-MEDIUM  
**Primary:** OBJECT / FORM  
**Secondary:** PHYSICS / INTERACTION  
**Tertiary:** MATERIAL / DETAIL

## Core formula

`cheap repeated parts -> new structural material -> invisible physical force -> impossible-looking balance -> collectible artifact`

## Core lesson

> TURN INVISIBLE FORCES INTO VISIBLE DESIGN.

> Secondary: MULTIPLY THE ORDINARY UNTIL IT BECOMES MATERIAL.

## Object idea

Office staples, metal wire, a small magnet and a bead become a desk artifact in which a central relic visually hangs under the top arm. Magnetic attraction, distance and balance create the feeling of an impossible object.

## What Design Brain should learn

- Ask what physical properties the found materials can contribute, not only what shapes can be assembled.
- Part of a form can be distance, shadow, tension, magnetic field, balance, light or movement.
- ONE CHEAP PART x MANY = NEW MATERIAL.
- Contrast a strict modular structure with a handmade irregular relic.

## What Design Brain must NOT copy

- the exact cross-shaped relic
- the exact red bead
- the exact L-shaped stand
- staples as a mandatory material
- the exact magnet type
- concrete background
- rusty bunker aesthetic

## Material language

- staple strips

- magnet
