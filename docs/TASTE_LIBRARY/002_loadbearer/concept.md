**Difficulty:** MEDIUM

## Core formula

`simple physics -> structural trick -> visual narrative -> collectible design`

## Core lesson

> TURN STRUCTURAL NECESSITY INTO STORY.

## Object idea

A book support becomes a small physical scene. A heavy base, angled support and reclaimed wood carry the real load while a character silhouette makes it look as if the figure is holding the weight of the books.

## What Design Brain should learn

- Turn a real force already present in the function - weight, tension, pressure, balance - into the visible narrative.
- FUNCTION CAN BECOME THE VISUAL JOKE.
- A simple structure can carry most of the engineering while one expressive element creates identity.
- EVERY MATERIAL SHOULD EARN ITS PLACE.

## What Design Brain must NOT copy

- soldier/scavenger as a mandatory motif
- gas mask, weapons or radiation signs
- rust everywhere
- brown/orange bunker lighting
- post-apocalyptic book titles as a style rule

## Material language

- reclaimed wood
- dark steel
- rust / patina
- exposed bolts
- rough edges
