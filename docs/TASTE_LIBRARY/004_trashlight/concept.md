## Core formula

`real trash -> simple assembly -> light intervention -> small collectible lamp`

## Core lesson

> DON'T UPGRADE THE MATERIAL - REASSIGN ITS ROLE.

## Object idea

Five cheap found objects - an empty cigarette pack, capped screw, plastic cup, bottle cap and foil - are reassigned into a small lamp. The value comes from changing each part's role rather than disguising the trash.

## What Design Brain should learn

- The lowest-status waste can become a collectible object through clear role reassignment.
- Do not hide material provenance; the object should remain honestly made from waste.
- Simplicity can be strong when form, new function, readable character and provenance are clear.

## What Design Brain must NOT copy

- the exact cigarette pack
- the exact plastic cup
- the exact screw
- the exact lamp silhouette
- the exact bunker / rust / brown-light presentation

## Material language

- crumpled packaging

- crumpled foil

- glue / tape / hand assembly
