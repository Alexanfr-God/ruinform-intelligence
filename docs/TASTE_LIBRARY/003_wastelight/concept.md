## Core formula

`ordinary / discarded components -> minimal intervention -> new luminous form -> collectible object`

## Core lesson

> DON'T OVERDESIGN THE PARTS - RECOMPOSE THEM INTO A NEW PRESENCE.

## Object idea

A used corrugated hose and a simple light strip become a sculptural light object. The light is inserted through the hose and the hose is hand-shaped; the strength comes from recomposition rather than complex engineering.

## What Design Brain should learn

- Strong objects do not always require complex electronics, mechanics or fabrication.
- Find a good union between two ordinary parts and change their roles.
- Let light reveal material form from within instead of becoming decoration.

## What Design Brain must NOT copy

- the exact loop shape
- the exact white hose
- the exact warm LED strip
- the bunker setting
- dusty brown/orange atmosphere as a style rule

## Material language

- weathered translucent corrugated hose
- warm flexible LED strip
- slightly worn controller
- internal glow
- traces of time
